Two real defects were reproduced. First, P-C2-200-END produced a typographic width452.71875pt for available451.27559pt because of invisible trailing whitespace, aborting the whole export with itemExceedsPage. Regression cases cover Latin, Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese and emoji; genuinely over-wide emoji clusters remain rejected. Diagnostic prints were removed.

Second, Apple's fallback font maps 日 and ⽇ to the same glyph. Quartz's reverse ToUnicode map selected the radical, while PDFKit normalized it. This also causes Quartz to emit exact replacement text for ambiguous characters into marked-content streams. No global Unicode normalization or font/glyph substitution is performed. A focused test distinguishes both characters byte-for-byte and excludes object-replacement-character inflation.
