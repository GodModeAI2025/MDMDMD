This policy describes processing by Scriptum for iOS and iPadOS. Available features depend on the app version, permissions and device settings. Services that have not been enabled cannot be used.

## 2. Documents and local storage

Scriptum processes the text, images and other attachments you create or open. This includes titles, page structure, tags, writing goals, comments, replies, revisions, timestamps and settings. AI conversation history, tasks, authorisation decisions and usage budgets may also be stored locally.

These data support writing, organisation, checking, recovery and export. Local drafts, revisions and recovery copies help prevent data loss. We do not operate a separate document-storage service or AI proxy to which your manuscripts are sent by default.

Storage depends on the library you choose. A storage provider selected through the Files app has its own terms and privacy information. Exported files are passed to your chosen location or recipient; copies outside Scriptum are managed by those parties.

## 3. iCloud synchronisation and collaboration

If you activate an available iCloud connection, Apple processes the documents, attachments, metadata and technical identifiers required for synchronisation through iCloud/CloudKit. Pending changes may be transferred after setup without a new manual action each time.

When you share pages or Spaces, authorised participants receive access according to the sharing permissions. Shared content may include comments, replies, editing states and participant attribution. Recipients can make copies; subsequently revoking access does not reliably delete those copies. Private and shared data are not intended to form a generally public document collection.

Disconnecting iCloud does not automatically delete local documents or existing cloud copies. Apple's processing is governed by its [privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [CloudKit information](https://www.apple.com/privacy/features/). This policy does not promise end-to-end encryption for all CloudKit data.

## 4. Optional AI features

For proofreading, rewriting, summarisation and assistance, you choose an available connection: OpenAI or Anthropic with your own API key, an authorised ChatGPT connection, or Apple Private Cloud Compute. Scriptum does not silently switch providers.

When an AI task starts, the selected text, instructions and contextual information are sent to your chosen service. Context may include page titles, references, selected pages and conversation history. An explicitly configured and confirmed schedule can run such tasks later, subject to operating-system availability. Disable the schedule to prevent further scheduled transmissions.

Services also receive information needed for connection, authentication and billing. Requests go directly to the chosen service. Providers may process technical connection data, such as your IP address. Retention, model training and other uses depend on the connection, contract and provider settings. API-key use and a ChatGPT account may be subject to different terms.

Private Cloud Compute processes necessary requests through Apple's protected infrastructure. Apple states that request content processed there is not retained permanently; Apple's separate system-diagnostic and transparency settings still apply. Availability depends on factors including device, region and language. See [Apple Intelligence and privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/intelligence-engine/).

Further information: [OpenAI privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), [OpenAI business terms](https://openai.com/policies/business-terms/), [Anthropic privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [Anthropic Commercial Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms).

### Images with Image Playground

When Image Playground is available on your device, you can explicitly open Apple's system interface from the image-block dialog. Scriptum supplies neither manuscript text nor existing page images as initial context. You choose descriptions and any photos within the system interface; Apple's Apple Intelligence privacy information applies to that processing. Scriptum offers the Apple illustration, sketch and animation styles.

A confirmed PNG or JPEG result is initially held temporarily within the app. Only choosing “Insert generated image” saves it as an ordinary image attachment with the page; the rules above for local storage, optional iCloud synchronisation and export then apply. Cancelling without inserting creates no new image block.

## 5. Credentials and keys

API keys and saved ChatGPT authentication tokens are stored in Apple's device-bound Keychain. They are not exported with your documents or synchronised as part of the Scriptum library.

OpenAI handles ChatGPT sign-in. Scriptum may receive verified account identifiers, an email address supplied by the service, and registration and permission information. Sign-in does not give Scriptum general access to all ChatGPT conversations. Local sign-out does not delete your provider account or content already submitted to it.

## 6. Text checking and other connections

Integrated text checking uses operating-system services. Scriptum does not send text to a preconfigured public LanguageTool server.

If you configure your own LanguageTool server and start a check, that server receives the checked text and necessary language and checking parameters. The operator of your selected server determines retention and further processing.

Queries for available AI models or published pricing may connect to relevant provider sites. These queries support connection and task planning and do not contain manuscript text. Opening external links takes you to the relevant provider and its privacy terms.

## 7. Diagnostics, TestFlight and support

Scriptum does not use advertising tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may provide technical operational information and aggregate service statistics as part of its services.

For TestFlight, Apple and the development team may receive installation, session and crash information. Feedback you submit can include screenshots, messages and document content visible in them. Apple's processing is governed by its [privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you contact us, we process your contact details, message and voluntarily attached files to handle your request. This information is not automatically sent to us from your manuscripts.

## 8. Purposes, legal bases and international processing

Where we process personal data, providing requested app features and fulfilling contracts rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Support and secure troubleshooting rely, where necessary, on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our interest is a secure, reliable app. Legal obligations rely on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Where processing requires consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR applies.

Apple, OpenAI, Anthropic or a server operator you select may process data outside the European Economic Area. Applicable service privacy information and contracts describe the relevant transfer arrangements. We do not treat selecting a feature as blanket authorisation for every international transfer. Business users should consider the permissions and agreements required for their content.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Documents and saved settings remain according to your use and deletion choices. Trash, revisions, conversation history, drafts and backups may contain additional versions. Deleting a current page, signing out, disconnecting iCloud or uninstalling the app therefore does not mean that all copies or Keychain entries have been deleted.

Manage local libraries, backups, exported files, sharing and copies held by storage providers separately. Content already sent to AI services or checking servers is subject to their deletion and retention procedures. We retain support information according to the time needed to handle your request; necessary statutory retention and the defence of specific legal claims remain applicable.

## 10. Your rights and policy changes

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You may withdraw consent for future processing and complain to a data-protection supervisory authority. Contact us using the address in section 1 for requests concerning our processing. We cannot inspect or delete data held exclusively in your own storage without your assistance.

Scriptum does not make solely automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. AI suggestions support your writing; you decide whether to apply them.

We update this policy and its date when processing changes. Privacy information for independently selected services may change separately.
