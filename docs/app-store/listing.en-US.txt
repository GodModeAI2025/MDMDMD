Description
Scriptum brings your writing, notes, and manuscripts into one workspace on iPhone and iPad. Write in Markdown, organize pages by project, and prepare your work to share.

ROOM FOR YOUR WRITING
Organize pages and subpages in Spaces. Use tags, favorites, and search to find drafts and supporting material.

WRITE WITH FOCUS
Work with Markdown source, headings, and an outline. Word counts and writing goals help you follow your progress. Add images and tables to your text.

KEEP TRACK OF VERSIONS
Compare saved versions and restore earlier text. Trash and recovery features support your writing workflow.

FROM DRAFT TO DOCUMENT
Assemble pages into a manuscript in your preferred chapter order. Export to PDF, Word/DOCX, HTML, or ePub. Keep your Markdown source, and include the images used in your document in a Markdown package.

YOUR TEXT, YOUR LAYOUT
Adjust fonts, spacing, margins, and paper size. Check the layout in preview before sharing an export. For blogs, copy formatted text or create a package with images.

Local writing and organization do not require an AI-provider account.
